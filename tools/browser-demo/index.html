Memory and output

CPU tile concurrency follows reported logical CPUs and the selected aggregate inference budget. WebGPU processes one tile at a time. Finished pixels are encoded in strips, with bounded preview size and backpressure. No full-size float output is assembled.

Each CPU tile worker has a 64 MiB WASM cap and an 80 MiB reservation including transferred tile buffers. Tracked GPU buffers at 256 MiB, PNG encoding at 128 MiB and zenjpeg decoding at 512 MiB. Canvas, graphics-driver overhead and the browser itself are outside those caps. JPEG repair has a separate 1 GiB WASM cap; its worker exits before SR workers start.

PNG output streams into browser-local storage where supported. The fallback keeps at most 128 MiB of encoded data. Download your finished result for a durable copy. Transparency is composited on white; output is 8-bit sRGB.
